UniHL7

One engine and a small profile per sender normalize different HL7 v2 dialects into one canonical model, link patients across systems and produce FHIR R4.

Normalize all three in a row and the patient index merges them into one person.

Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded. The patient index lives only in this tab. Do not paste real patient data into any website.
